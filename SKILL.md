## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is a contemporary faux-naïf illustration that turns an ordinary subject into one playful emotional talisman. Centred, iconic, one rough silhouette, abundant quiet space, naïve but intentional. Flat candy-coloured shapes, imperfect hand-drawn contours, loose crayon and oil-pastel scribble shading, visible hand pressure. The ground is deep, misty and matte; the subject is lighter and more alive. Two to four source colours, reorganised to feel warm, odd and slightly magical, candy but not sickly. A few tiny abstract sparks or glyphs may orbit. One expressive non-realistic ground shadow. It is not a polished vector, a realistic volume study, a cute commercial character, a filled scene or a smooth digital finish.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-242/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
